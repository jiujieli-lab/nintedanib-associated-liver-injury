Figure S14. Molecular-property predictions and nintedanib receptor-state sensitivity.
(A–C) Predicted logD at pH 7.4, acidic pKa and basic pKa for all 16 prespecified parent structures. Points are model estimates; segments are provider-returned model bounds with unspecified coverage, not confidence intervals. The index drug is highlighted. Open symbols denote out-of-domain flags, and diamonds denote low-confidence predictions; an open diamond identifies both flags. Predictions used mcp_public_logd version 1.7.0 and mcp_public_apka/mcp_public_bpka version 1.4.0, with all input chemical identities verified. (D) Nintedanib PDGFRA poses 1 and 3 from seed 104729 are shown together, retaining the alternative geometries despite their small score difference. Annotated distances refer to Thr674, the gatekeeper residue, and do not establish a canonical hinge interaction. (E) The nintedanib top-ranked FLT4 pose from the same seed is displayed in the predicted AlphaFold receptor; selected labels are heavy-atom proximity distances below 4 Å, not assigned hydrogen bonds. (F) Separate nintedanib-only receptor sensitivity compares the three primary 6JOL searches with three additional apo 8PQJ searches under the same specified seed and docking settings. Top-pose Vina scores and minimum Cys677 heavy-atom distances are shown. The 8PQJ comparison was retained separately and did not replace any of the 96 primary library searches or their rankings. Points are computational searches, not independent biological replicates; no inferential tests were performed across seeds. The 8PQJ preparation repaired 33 missing heavy/terminal atoms without moving deposited atoms. Full receptor-preparation methods, all 53 retained sensitivity poses, and run-level values are available in the source-data archive. Neither physicochemical predictions nor docking establishes DILI risk, hepatoprotection, or therapeutic benefit.
