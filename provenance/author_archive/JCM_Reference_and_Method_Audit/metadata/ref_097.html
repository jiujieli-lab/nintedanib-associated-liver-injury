Add any organism to STRING / Annotate proteome
Upload a complete species proteome to STRING, and we'll generate its interaction network and predict protein functions, including Gene Ontology terms and KEGG pathways. Once uploaded, you can explore and analyze your proteome data through our web interface, access it programmatically via API, or download all predictions in bulk.
