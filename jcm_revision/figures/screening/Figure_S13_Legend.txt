Figure S13. Complete seeded docking profiles and biochemical evidence coverage.
(A,B) Actual Vina scores from all 96 primary searches: 16 compounds, two receptors and three prespecified seeds (104729, 130363, 155921). Scores and color scales are interpreted within each receptor. PDGFRA used experimental 6JOL; FLT4 used a predicted AlphaFold receptor. (C,D) Maximum pairwise symmetry-aware heavy-atom RMSD among the three top-ranked poses, calculated in the receptor coordinate frame without ligand superposition. (E) Eligible exact biochemical record counts, separated by receptor and Kd, Ki or IC50; a dash means no eligible record was available, not demonstrated inactivity. (F) All 30 retained PDGFRA nintedanib poses across the three seeds, with larger dark-outlined symbols identifying each seed's top-ranked pose. Distances are minimum heavy-atom proximities to Thr674, the gatekeeper residue. The dashed 4 Å line is a proximity reference and does not establish a hydrogen bond or canonical hinge interaction. Repeated search seeds are computational consistency checks, not biological replicates; no significance tests treat them as experiments. Docking scores are not calibrated affinities or evidence of hepatoprotection. Full run-level values, receptor definitions, compound identities, record dispositions and pose files are retained in the source-data tables and code archive.
