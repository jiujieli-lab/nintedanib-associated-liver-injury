**Figure 6 | Public preclinical perturbations define pulmonary-response and tissue-context boundaries.** **A**, Principal-component analysis of TMM-normalized lung transcriptomes from GSE278200; each point represents one independent rat (SAL, BLM, and BLM plus nintedanib, *n*=4 per group). **B**, Disease effects (BLM versus SAL) plotted against treatment effects (nintedanib versus BLM) for 3,647 disease-responsive genes (disease *q*<0.05 and |log2 fold change|≥0.5). The dashed line denotes complete reversal (*y*=−*x*). For directionally reversed genes, the reversal index was defined as −(treatment log2 fold change)/(disease log2 fold change); its distribution is descriptive because genes are correlated. **C**, Animal-level nintedanib-versus-BLM differential expression in GSE278200. Benjamini–Hochberg correction was performed across 13,598 expressed genes. **D**, Moderated log2 fold changes and 95% confidence intervals for prespecified targets, metabolic anchors, and fibrosis genes in GSE278200; FGFR3 was below the expression filter. **E**, Nintedanib effects on seven prespecified GSE278200 module scores. Points show between-group differences in mean gene-standardized module scores; bars show Welch 95% confidence intervals. Multiplicity-controlled inference used all 70 exact label permutations and Benjamini–Hochberg correction across modules. **F**, Principal-component analysis of GSE308578 independent-mouse lung transcriptomes (CTRL, *n*=10; BLM plus vehicle, *n*=16; BLM plus nintedanib, *n*=17). GEO SOFT and the source ARRIVE table agree on these group sizes. **G**, Disease-versus-treatment effect geometry for 3,499 GSE308578 disease-responsive genes. The high directional reversal fraction occurred at a small median reversal index, indicating lower-magnitude transcriptomic restoration. **H**, Nintedanib-versus-vehicle effects for prespecified genes in GSE308578. Points use all animals; horizontal bars span 33 leave-one-relevant-animal-out estimates. OTC, GSTA1, and FBP1 were below the lung expression filter. **I**, Exploratory slice-level effects in GSE120804 BDL rat liver PCLSs after 1.0 µM nintedanib for 48 h (10 pooled-tissue slice observations per displayed group). Purple indicates slice-level genome-wide *q*<0.05. Tissues from three rats were pooled before slice randomization; these values therefore do not support animal-population inference and do not model drug-induced liver injury. **J**, Cross-model nintedanib-versus-disease gene or protein effects and unweighted means of observed gene/protein effects within each displayed module. Grey denotes an unobserved feature. GSE120679 comprises pooled-tissue rat lung PCLS observations (six per group), and PXD024058 protein effects are descriptive because the source is internally inconsistent about biological versus technical replicate provenance. Lung datasets define a pulmonary-response compatibility boundary, not evidence of hepatic safety or toxicity. NINT, nintedanib; BLM, bleomycin; PCLS, precision-cut tissue slice; TMM, trimmed mean of M values.
