# Supplementary Figure S7. Unconditioned animal-level lung-proteome response to nintedanib in bleomycin-exposed mice (PXD052594).

Role convention: Fgfr1 is the prespecified exposure-proximal target track displayed in this lung dataset; Fbp1 is a P1 DILI phenotype anchor and Gsta1/Otc are P2 DILI phenotype anchors, not drug targets.

All remaining animals in the deposited processed proteome were included (n=10 nintedanib; n=13 vehicle); no radiomic response cluster was used in sample selection or modeling. **(A)** Number of quantified proteins per individual animal. **(B)** PCA of the 2,000 most variable proteins. **(C)** All-animal nintedanib-versus-vehicle protein contrast; the dashed line denotes nominal P=0.05 and no protein met BH q<0.10. **(D)** Standardized abundances for the 20 smallest nominal protein P values; display selection is not an inferential threshold. **(E)** Nintedanib-minus-vehicle differences in five prespecified protein-program scores with Welch 95% confidence intervals; no module met BH significance. **(F–H)** Individual-animal extracellular-matrix, macrophage-repair, and RTK-response module scores, respectively; black bars show mean ± SEM for visualization. **(I)** Protein log2 fold changes and 95% confidence intervals for the exposure-proximal Fgfr1 target, downstream DILI phenotype anchors, and lung phenotype tracks; crosses denote proteins not quantified. **(J)** Absolute deposited log2 protein abundance for the same tracks; gray rows denote non-quantified proteins. The source randomized 15 mice per arm, but six postrandomization exclusions occurred before the final imaging cohort and one additional vehicle was excluded upstream from comparative proteomics. Accordingly, the figure is interpreted as a pulmonary contextual sensitivity and not as unbiased causal confirmation or hepatic-safety evidence. The separate 5-versus-5 phosphosite analysis is a table-only, unmoderated Welch–BH screen of deposited processed intensities and is not an exact small-sample test or an occupancy analysis.
