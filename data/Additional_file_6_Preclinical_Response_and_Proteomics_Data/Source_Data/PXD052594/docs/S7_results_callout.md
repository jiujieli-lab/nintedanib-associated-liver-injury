### Unconditioned animal-level PXD052594 sensitivity

An unconditioned analysis of every animal in the deposited PXD052594 processed lung proteome (10 nintedanib and 13 vehicle mice) found no protein at BH q<0.10 and no prespecified protein module or candidate track that survived within-family BH false-discovery-rate control (Figure S7). Fgfr1 abundance was null, while the downstream Fbp1, Gsta1, and Otc DILI phenotype anchors were not quantified. A separate random 5-versus-5 phosphoproteome subset produced 994 of 20,043 sites at BH q<0.05 in an unmoderated Welch–BH screen, but changes were broadly bidirectional and no quantified canonical RTK-pathway site met BH q<0.05. Because only deposited processed intensities were available, no exact small-sample test, matched total-protein adjustment, or site-occupancy model was possible; site shifts cannot be separated from protein-abundance shifts and are not interpreted as signaling-state or causal-target confirmation. Because six mice were excluded after randomization and a further vehicle proteome was removed for sample-preparation problems, PXD052594 provides a nonconfirmatory pulmonary pharmacodynamic sensitivity rather than unbiased causal or hepatic-safety evidence.
