# GSE151374 pooled-library reanalysis: frozen Methods, Results, and Figure S6 legend

## Source and evidence boundary

GSE151374 (BioProject PRJNA635636; SRA study SRP265115) was downloaded from the NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE151374; accessed 4 September 2026). The record became public on 5 May 2021 and was last updated on 2 February 2024. The linked primary report is Watson et al., *American Journal of Respiratory Cell and Molecular Biology* 2023;68:366–380 (PMID 36227799; doi:10.1165/rcmb.2022-0021OC). The experiment comprised male C57BL/6J mice in six condition–time cells: saline control, bleomycin (BLM), or BLM plus nintedanib at days 7 and 14. Each cell contained three independently prepared 10x libraries, and each library pooled lungs from three mice (18 libraries; 54 mice represented). Nintedanib was administered at 50 mg/kg on days 0–6 for day-7 collection and days 7–13 for day-14 collection. Because the GEO record used for this reanalysis does not specify the BLM dose or route, those fields are not inferred here. This dataset constrains pulmonary pharmacodynamic and immune-context interpretation; it does not measure liver injury or establish hepatic safety.

## Methods

We downloaded the 18 deposited Cell Ranger unfiltered HDF5 count matrices and verified the accession-to-condition map against the GEO SOFT record. The sole inferential unit was the pooled 10x library (n=3 libraries per condition at each time point); neither individual cells nor the three mice combined within a library were treated as independent observations. Barcodes were retained when they contained at least 500 unique molecular identifiers (UMIs), 200–6,000 detected genes, and less than 20% mitochondrial UMIs. Broad lineages were assigned solely to support library-level aggregation. For every retained cell, we calculated the mean log1p(CP10K) expression across prespecified lineage markers for macrophage, monocyte, neutrophil, dendritic, T, B, NK, epithelial, and endothelial compartments. A barcode was labeled “Other” when the largest marker score was below 0.10 or exceeded the second-largest score by less than 0.05. Marker-score separation and aggregate marker expression were exported as annotation checks.

Raw counts were summed within each library for all retained cells and, separately, for the marker-defined macrophage stratum. Counts were converted to log2 counts per million with a 0.5 prior count. Principal-component analysis used the 2,000 most variable genes across library-level pseudobulks. Day-matched BLM+nintedanib versus BLM macrophage contrasts retained genes with CPM at least 1 in at least three of the six libraries and used two-sided Welch t tests; Benjamini–Hochberg (BH) correction was applied separately within each day. These gene-level analyses were considered exploratory because each contrast contained only three libraries per arm.

Cell-type proportions were calculated within each pooled library. Five macrophage programs were prespecified (repair, inflammatory, extracellular-matrix remodeling, alveolar homeostasis, and receptor-tyrosine-kinase response). A program score was the mean of gene-wise standardized macrophage-pseudobulk logCPM values among detected member genes. For proportions and programs, we report the difference in library means, a Welch 95% confidence interval, Hedges' *g*, a two-sided Welch test, and the exhaustive two-sided label-permutation test over all 20 possible 3-versus-3 allocations. BH correction was applied across all displayed cell-type-by-day tests or the ten module-by-day tests, respectively. Because the exact two-sided permutation grid has a minimum attainable P value of 0.10, effect sizes, intervals, and all three pool values are emphasized. Candidate genes were analyzed analogously at the pooled-library level. All analyses were run in Python 3.12.13 (NumPy 2.3.5, pandas 2.2.3, SciPy 1.17.0, scikit-learn 1.8.0, matplotlib 3.10.8, and h5py 3.16.0); exact software and file hashes are recorded in `results/analysis_manifest.json` and independently checked in `validation/manifest_hash_validation.json`.

## Results

The deterministic quality-control rule retained 70,696 cells across 18 pooled libraries (2,841–6,188 per library); every library retained more than 100 macrophage-stratum cells. All-cell and macrophage pseudobulk ordinations separated uninjured controls from BLM-exposed samples and showed substantial time- and pool-level heterogeneity, supporting analysis at the library rather than cell level (Figure S6A–F).

At day 14, the macrophage fraction was 0.37435 in BLM+nintedanib libraries and 0.28517 in BLM libraries, a difference of 0.08918 (95% CI, −0.05667 to 0.23503; Hedges' *g*=1.125; Welch P=0.1627; exhaustive permutation P=0.20; cell-type-family BH q=0.5937). The corresponding day-7 difference was 0.00176 (95% CI, −0.09236 to 0.09588; *g*=0.036; Welch P=0.9588; permutation P=0.90). Thus, the independent-pool estimates were directionally compatible with late macrophage expansion but did not provide multiplicity-controlled confirmation (Figure S6D,E).

No macrophage gene-level contrast survived BH correction (12,520 tested features at day 7, minimum q=0.583; 11,945 at day 14, minimum q=0.990; Figure S6G,H). Among the five prespecified programs, the day-14 receptor-tyrosine-kinase-response score was 0.49480 higher with nintedanib (95% CI, 0.09485–0.89474; *g*=3.018; Welch P=0.03174; permutation P=0.10), but multiplicity correction was not met (Welch-BH q=0.317; permutation-BH q=0.75). All other program-by-day contrasts likewise had q>0.05 (Figure S6I).

Within macrophage pseudobulks, *Fgfr1* was detected in all six day-14 libraries and was 1.18594 log2CPM units higher with nintedanib than with BLM alone (95% CI, 0.14117–2.23072; *g*=2.486; Welch P=0.03708; exact permutation P=0.10; candidate-family BH q=0.742). This is a pharmacodynamic lung-context observation, not evidence that higher FGFR1 causes or protects against hepatic injury. The liver-enriched candidate tracks showed the expected lack of informative macrophage-lung signal: *Gsta1* was absent from all macrophage pseudobulks, *Fbp1* was detected only sparsely, and *Otc* was absent or sporadic (Figure S6J). Collectively, GSE151374 supports a pulmonary macrophage/RTK response boundary while remaining non-informative for direct nintedanib hepatotoxicity.

Role convention: *Fgfr1* is the sole exposure-proximal target track; *Fbp1* is a downstream P1 DILI phenotype anchor and *Gsta1*/*Otc* are downstream P2 DILI phenotype anchors, not drug targets.

**Figure S6. Pooled-library single-cell reanalysis of nintedanib response in bleomycin-exposed mouse lung (GSE151374).** Each plotted inferential observation is one independently prepared 10x library pooling three mice (n=3 libraries per condition and time point); cells are never treated as independent replicates. **(A)** Number of QC-retained cells in each pooled library. **(B)** Median UMI count versus median detected genes among retained cells, summarized once per library. **(C)** Principal-component analysis of all-retained-cell library pseudobulks. Circles indicate day 7 and squares day 14. **(D)** Broad marker-rule lineage composition of every pooled library. **(E)** Macrophage-stratum fractions in day-matched BLM and BLM+nintedanib libraries; points are pooled libraries and black bars show mean ± SEM for visualization. Inferential statistics use pooled-library proportions. **(F)** Principal-component analysis of macrophage-stratum pseudobulks. **(G,H)** Exploratory macrophage-pseudobulk gene-level contrasts of BLM+nintedanib versus BLM at days 7 and 14, respectively; the dashed line denotes nominal P=0.05. Two-sided Welch tests were BH-corrected within day; no feature met q<0.10. **(I)** Nintedanib-minus-BLM differences in five prespecified macrophage program scores, with Welch 95% confidence intervals; all three pool values underlie each group estimate, and exhaustive 3-versus-3 permutation tests are provided in the source table. **(J)** Absolute log2(CPM+0.5) abundance of prespecified target and phenotype tracks in macrophage pseudobulks; the low/absent *Fbp1*, *Gsta1*, and *Otc* rows are retained as explicit detectability results. This experiment provides pulmonary-response context only and is not a liver-toxicity experiment.
