### Independent-pool sensitivity analysis of GSE151374

Reanalysis of GSE151374 at the deposited pooled-library unit (n=3 10x libraries per condition and time point; three mice pooled per library) retained 70,696 cells but produced no genome-wide macrophage differential-expression result at BH q<0.05 and no composition, prespecified module, or candidate-track result that survived exact-permutation BH correction (minimum attainable two-sided exact P=0.10; Figure S6). The day-14 nintedanib groups showed directionally higher macrophage abundance and RTK-response scores, but uncertainty was wide and multiplicity-controlled evidence was absent. We therefore use this dataset only as a nonconfirmatory pulmonary pharmacodynamic boundary, not as validation of hepatic toxicity or a causal liver target.
